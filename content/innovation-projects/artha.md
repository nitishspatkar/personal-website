## Artha – Real‑Time Water Analytics

Artha develops an AI‑enhanced water monitoring and analytics platform that combines automated sampling modules with an integrated Deep UV spectrometer for simultaneous Raman and fluorescence analyses, featuring low‑maintenance design and optional self‑cleaning capabilities.

Their proprietary Spectrum Analyzer software, built on Google TensorFlow, leverages full‑spectrum fluorescence analysis and machine learning to process multiple parameters in real time for comprehensive substance identification.

Designed for water utilities, wastewater treatment plants, and environmental protection agencies, Artha’s solution provides real‑time alerts, threshold monitoring, and actionable insights to improve operational safety and accelerate emergency response.

Rooted in a mission to deliver precise measurement methods and high‑quality data for process optimization and environmental sustainability, Artha empowers industries to save resources and contribute to a cleaner environment.

To eliminate on‑site analysis bottlenecks, we introduced a cloud‑hosted web application—selected via a MAUT evaluation of cost, maintenance, AI‑integration, availability, and data residency—that automatically uploads sensor data to a scalable database. Users and system administrators can then access, visualize, and compare measurements through customizable dashboards from any browser within minutes of acquisition. The platform’s modular design supports future extensions for AI‑based interpretation and predictive analytics, empowering Artha’s clients with rapid, remote insights into water‑quality trends and anomalies.
