## Overview
CEEX is a Swiss AI‑driven software provider offering two flagship platforms—**Flex Grid** for Distribution System Operators and **Power Community** for local energy communities—to optimize grid stability, integrate renewables and automate energy flows in real time.

## Flex Grid (for DSOs)
- **Data Engineering:** Ingests and harmonizes smart‑meter, weather and market data for precise, real‑time analytics  
- **AI‑Powered Forecasts:** Bottom‑up load and PV production predictions, plus congestion and voltage‑overload warnings  
- **Dynamic Pricing:** Real‑time network tariffs and energy prices that incentivize load shifting and reduce balancing costs  
- **Automated Dispatch Management:** Intelligent control of energy flows to avoid bottlenecks and maximize asset utilization  
- **Decentralized Flexibility Integration:** Orchestrates batteries, demand‑response and EV charging to bolster grid resilience  

## Power Community (for Local Energy Communities)
- **Data Engineering:** Consolidates community generation and consumption metrics for transparent decision‑making  
- **Dynamic Pricing:** Real‑time price signals that balance supply and demand while prioritizing renewables  
- **Energy Marketplace:** Decentralized trading platform for local peer‑to‑peer energy exchanges  
- **Decentralized Flexibilities:** Leverages local flexibility resources to maximize self‑consumption and support the grid  

## Technology & Benefits
- **Proactive Grid Management:** Anticipate and mitigate network bottlenecks before they occur  
- **Cost Reduction:** Lower balancing‑energy and operational expenses via automated pricing and dispatch  
- **Renewable Integration:** Increase the share of renewables and reduce CO₂ emissions through smart flex‑resource scheduling  
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Ensure continuous alignment with contractual and legal requirements
