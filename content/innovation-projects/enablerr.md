Pier4all AG is a Swiss software company based in Technopark Lucerne that develops and operates the enablerr Business Solution Eco‑System—a community‑based, no‑code platform that auto‑generates interfaces and data models without programming. Enablerr is a fully configurable, web‑based system designed to integrate seamlessly into existing infrastructures, adapt to unique workflows via plug‑ins, and scale with growing business needs. Pier4all was honored as “Startup of the Week” by the Swiss Startup Association in April 2021 and is a member of the Swiss Made Software association, underscoring its Swiss‑quality standards. The underlying “Next Generation ERP” research project ran from January 2021 to April 2023 with CHF 595,341.94 in federal funding for applied research and development.
