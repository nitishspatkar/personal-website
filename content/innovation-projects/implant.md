Andreas Mitterer is the key person behind Mitterer Technologies and a Swiss engineer specializing in the design and development of active implants that deliver electrostimulation, biosignal sensing, wireless energy and data transfer through intact skin, and IoT cloud connectivity for remote fitting via web and mobile applications. 
He has extensive leadership experience in regulated medical device development, serving as VP of R&D at Implantica and Senior Director of Design & Engineering at Flex, where he managed cross‑functional teams under MDR and FDA regulations. 
Earlier in his career, he led design teams at MED‑EL in Innsbruck for implantable neuro‑stimulation and cochlear hearing devices, overseeing projects from concept through mass production. 
His academic foundation in chemical process and environmental engineering, along with research in quantum optics under Nobel laureate Prof. Anton Zeilinger, underpins his multidisciplinary approach to tackling biocompatibility and electro‑corrosion challenges in active implant design. 

Together with his company, we are developing a novel platform to implant management.
