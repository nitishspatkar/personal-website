### Short Biography
I am currently a senior researcher at FHNW (University of Applied Sciences and Arts, Northwestern Switzerland) within the [Institute for Interactive Technologies](https://www.fhnw.ch/en/about-fhnw/schools/school-of-engineering/institutes/institute-for-interactive-technologies).

I completed my Ph.D. at the University of Bern. Between 2018 and 2022, I was a member of the [Software Composition Group](https://scg.unibe.ch/) led by Prof. Oscar Nierstrasz. My work primarily focuses on software and requirements engineering, with a strong interest in UX, digital sustainability, applied AI, and improving agile way of development. 

Discover my doctoral research thesis, titled [Supporting Multiple Stakeholders in Agile Development](./moldable-requirements.md).
